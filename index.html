FAQ

Good to know

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How do I start a project?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 92093 39989, email synqwave.business@gmail.com, or fill in the form below. We'll reply with a few questions or a quote.

What are your payment terms?

We take a 50% advance to start the work. The balance is due before we hand over the final files.

Who owns the final designs?

You do. Ownership of the final designs transfers to you once the final payment is made.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the job, but here's what we usually take (working days, after we have your brief and content):

  • Social media posts and stories1–2 days
  • Reels2–3 days
  • Visiting cards, flyers, posters and menus2–3 days
  • Logo design, including first concepts4–6 days
  • Packaging and labels5–7 days
  • Full brand identity10–15 days
  • Monthly packages: we plan the month's calendar with you in week one and deliver on a schedule.

Need it urgently? Tell us; we'll be honest about whether we can do it. We confirm the exact timeline with every quote.

Is printing included?

No. Our quotes cover design only. Printing cost is separate and depends on size, material and quantity.

What if I need more than two revision rounds?

Two revision rounds are included. After that, each extra revision is ₹50.
