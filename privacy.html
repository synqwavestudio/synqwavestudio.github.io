Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the details you share with us.

What we receive

Only what you choose to send us: for example your name, business name, the service you need, your budget range, your message, and your phone number or email address when you write to us.

How the contact form works

The form on this website doesn't store anything. It opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to work on your project.

What we don't do

We don't sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing.

Contact

To ask what we have, or to have it deleted, email synqwave.business@gmail.com.
